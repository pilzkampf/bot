# Multiple Groups

You can join multiple groups where you play with the Pilzkampf Bot. We call this a "federation". One bot, multiple groups, different communities.
These can be different languages, time zones, or friend groups. When the bot is active in multiple groups, you don't need to create
a new account in the other group. You can post new mushrooms in every group. Each group, as well as each user, can have different languages and time zones.

This allows you not only to make your mushrooms available to another group, but you can also find new friends!

## Federating

When you offer a mushroom, you can also offer *the same mushroom* in another group. With the command
`/publish` you can publish your current mushrooms in another group that has the mushroom bot. People in the other group
can react to the mushroom just like in your home group.

## Home Group

When you use the `/pilz` command in a group, the mushroom will be published there. Each group has different members, languages, and time zones.
If you want to define your home group, you can do this under `/userconfig` in the group settings. This is ***the*** group where
your mushroom will be published when you do it in the bot chat.

## Privacy

When you post your mushroom in other groups, the Telegram contacts of the participants are not shared if they are not also in the group. It's important that
the mushroom creator can contact the players. We value privacy and don't share data unless necessary.

You can recognize mushrooms from other groups by the globe emoji 🌎. Players who are in the mushroom from other groups also have this globe emoji 🌎.
